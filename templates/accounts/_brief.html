Decision rehearsal for operators and founders

Test the business before reality does.

  1. IDEAStress-test pricing, demand, CAC and costs across best, expected and worst cases, and see which assumption moves the outcome most.
  2. OPSKnock out the payment gateway, the warehouse or the courier, and compare waiting it out against switching over or queueing the backlog.
  3. BRIEFRecord who decided what, on which numbers, and print the brief.

Every figure comes from your own inputs and a deterministic model you can inspect. AI analysis is labelled as such. Nothing here predicts the market or guarantees an outcome.
